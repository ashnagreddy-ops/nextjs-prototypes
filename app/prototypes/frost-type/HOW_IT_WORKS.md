# Frost Type

A typing experiment. Each typed character appears right away as an ice letter. Icicles and snow caps grow on it a moment later, then carved filigree scrollwork grows inside it, the way plants grow out of letters in Type Garden. There is no backspace/melt or space behaviour yet.

## How it works

- **Glyphs.** Each keypress creates a glyph with its own character, seed (`hashSeed(globalSeed, index)`), and birth time. Building a glyph renders the letter onto its own offscreen canvas. From that canvas it takes a mask (coverage + solid), a chamfer distance field, and edge normals. It then precomputes its icicles, snow caps, and filigree from separate seeded random streams. Layers are device-pixel canvases in the glyph's local box: the ice body, icicles + snow, two filigree layers (light and shadow), and a clip canvas. When everything has finished, they're flattened into one cached canvas.
- **Per-glyph clocks.** Each element has a start time and duration relative to its glyph's birth, so the visual state is a pure function of age. Fast typing never delays anything. A rebuild (resize, size change, Shift+R) regenerates the same geometry and catches up to the current age instantly.
- **Timeline.** `BODY_DELAY` 0 with a 250ms freeze-in: the body is revealed through a clip of circles expanding from two deep interior points. Then `ICICLE_DELAY` 200, `SNOW_DELAY` 350, and `FILIGREE_DELAY` 500, each staggered per element, all with an ease-out cubic.
- **Ice body.** A vertical `ICE_LIGHT` → `ICE_MID` → `ICE_DEEP` gradient at 0.92 alpha, then (drawn `source-atop` so it stays inside the letter) a blurred `ICE_DEEP` outline stroke for thick glassy edges and ~12 soft white streaks warped by seeded noise. Thin rims come from subtracting the glyph from a copy of itself shifted 1–2px: `ICE_HIGHLIGHT` on upper-left edges, `OUTLINE` on lower-right. The blur uses `ctx.filter`, so browsers without it just skip the softening.
- **Icicles.** Sampled at 5–9px jittered spacing along every downward-facing run (arch undersides and counter ceilings included), gated by low-frequency value noise so there are bare patches and clusters. `len = MIN + (MAX-MIN)·r³` (MAX ≈ 0.28 × glyph height), clipped to the free space below. Each is a slightly curved spike with an `ICE_LIGHT` gradient (0.85 → 0.15 alpha), a highlight line on the left edge and an `OUTLINE` line on the right; about 20% end in a droplet bead. Short ones grow in 450ms, long ones in 1300ms.
- **Snow.** White `SNOW` drifts on upward-facing runs (normal.y < -0.7) with a `SNOW_SHADOW` copy 2.5px below. Thickness varies 3–10px by seeded noise, and a few 1–2px sparkle dots sit on the surface.
- **Filigree (a rinceau).** `skeleton.ts` thins the glyph mask (Zhang-Suen, which leaves the ridge of the distance field) into a 1px skeleton, traces it into edges between endpoints and junctions (adjacent junction pixels are unioned), prunes spurs (anything under 12px, or a short branch that runs out to the glyph edge, which are corner artifacts), merges edges through plain junctions, then simplifies (RDP) and smooths (Catmull-Rom) each into a **spine**. `filigree.ts` draws each spine as a bold vine (width = 12% of local thickness, clamped 3–9px, tapered at free tips), then:
  - finishes each free tip with a **terminal spiral** (about 15% of those on edge-adjacent tips spill up to 14px past the silhouette, on an unclipped layer);
  - walks the spine emitting a **scroll** every 1.1 x local thickness, strictly alternating sides. A scroll is a stem leaving at ~50° that curves outward, then an Archimedean spiral of 1.5–2.25 turns wound in the direction mirroring its side. Sizes follow a large-small-medium-small rhythm (±10%). Each candidate must clear `EDGE_MARGIN` against the distance field and a 2px **occupancy grid** (every drawn stroke is marked; a scroll that touches occupied cells shrinks 15% and retries up to 4 times, then is skipped). Later passes fill the gaps with smaller scrolls until `TARGET_COVERAGE` or nothing fits;
  - only afterwards adds **C-curls** on stems of the larger scrolls, **teardrop leaves** in ~1 of 4 gaps, and **beads** at scroll junctions and some spiral centres.
- **Placement details.** Occupancy is a 1px grid that marks each drawn stroke's width + 1px (nothing wider), and a scroll ignores occupancy only for its first ~2px past its own spine. The edge margin is `min(EDGE_MARGIN, 0.12 x local thickness)`. Base scroll radius is 0.42 x thickness, shrinking 15% per retry for up to 6 retries (to ~40%). After the two spine passes, a **pocket pass** repeatedly finds the largest empty circle (a chamfer clearance field over unoccupied cells, six circles per field) and winds a smaller spiral into it, anchored to the nearest stroke, until `TARGET_COVERAGE`. Set `DEBUG_FILIGREE` in `config.ts` to log per-glyph spines, candidates, rejects (by reason) and coverage.
- **Breaking the silhouette.** About 25% of free spine tips and scroll stems run on past the edge by 10–24px and curl back; every glyph gets 2–3 tendrils that start outside and curve in over a serif or shoulder, and 2–4 snowflakes centred on sharp convex corners (found by the solid fraction of a small disk). All of these go on the unclipped spill layer. Flakes use the three-stroke relief (no glow) and bloom with ease-out-back about 900ms after `FILIGREE_DELAY`; since ink is never erased, the overshoot sticks as slightly longer arms.
- **Body depth.** 4–6 faint facet lines (offset copies of the spines, white at 0.10–0.18) and 6–10 tiny trapped bubbles are drawn into the body, which sits at 0.85 alpha.
- **Carved relief.** `relief.ts` paints every stroke, leaf and bead onto four stacked layers (glow, shadow, body gradient, highlight) as opaque colour. Alpha and the 3px glow blur are applied when compositing, so overlapping joins never bead. Strokes are clipped to the glyph (dilated 1px) except spill strokes.
- **Growth and incremental paint.** Spines ease out over 1.2–1.6s. A scroll starts when its spine's tip passes its attachment point: the stem extends (250ms ease-out), then the spiral winds in (500ms ease-in-out). Leaves scale in after their neighbouring scrolls (250ms) and beads pop with a small overshoot. All geometry is precomputed once; each frame paints only the newly revealed slice of each stroke onto the glyph's layers (leaves and beads only ever add ink as they scale up), never clearing.
- **Layout.** The font size shrinks with length until 12 characters fit in 90vw, then text wraps, shrinking further only if the rows overflow vertically. Cached glyphs are drawn scaled while the size drifts and are rebuilt (2 per frame) once the scale leaves 0.8–1.08.
- **Performance.** Finished glyphs cost one `drawImage`. The main canvas is redrawn only on frames where something changed. If more than 2000 elements are animating, the oldest glyphs are fast-forwarded to finished.
- **Keys.** Letters, numbers, and basic punctuation type; modifier combos and key repeat are ignored. **Shift+R** regenerates all glyphs with a new seed and replays them. A plain `r` types a letter. All tunables are in `config.ts`. Canvas colours are literals because canvas can't read CSS tokens. The page background is the `BACKGROUND` constant (dark navy) rather than a theme token, because the ice palette is tuned for it. Font: `FONT_FAMILY` (Abril Fatface) via `next/font`, with Playfair Display 900 as the fallback if it fails to load; `FORCE_UPPERCASE` is off.

## Key files

- `page.tsx` — canvas + "type something" hint
- `scene.ts` — input, frame loop, rebuild scheduling, active-element cap
- `text-layout.ts` — font size, wrapping, pen positions (not `layout.ts`, which Next.js would treat as a route layout)
- `glyph.ts` — glyph build, ice body, freeze-in, per-frame update, baking
- `mask.ts` — mask, distance field, normals, surface runs
- `skeleton.ts` — mask → spine polylines
- `filigree.ts`, `icicles.ts`, `snow.ts` — the three growth systems (`filigree.ts` builds the scroll geometry)
- `relief.ts` — carved-relief layers, element painting and per-element growth
- `font.ts` — resolved font family and weight for canvas (the page loads it via `next/font` and waits for it before starting)
- `stroke.ts` (polyline/easing helpers), `rng.ts`, `color.ts`, `config.ts` — helpers and tunables

## Open questions

- Should plain R regenerate instead (and lose typing lowercase r)?
- Mobile has no keyboard: add a hidden input to summon one?
- Scrolls are small on narrow strokes: a spiral has to fit between the spine and the glyph edge. Bigger text (fewer letters) gets visibly larger scrolls.
