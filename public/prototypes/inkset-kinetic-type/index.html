Every element on this page moves on one of four primitives — stagger-drop, press-and-settle, flex-on-hover, snap-transition. Nothing fades. Weight is the only thing that changes on hover; the accent is reserved for surfaces that respond to touch. Turn sound on to hear the mechanical layer.
